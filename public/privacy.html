Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 2, 2026

This page explains what we collect when you apply for the closed beta of THE SYSTEM: AWAKENING (developed by Axon), why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Your Discord username
  • Your device platform (Android, iOS or both)
  • Your answers to the questionnaire (hunter path, expected activity, bug-reporting commitment, how you found us)
  • Your email address, only if you choose to give it
  • The date and time you applied

We don't ask for your real name, location or payment details.

Why we collect it

Only to run the closed beta: to choose testers, invite them (on Discord, or by email as a backup) and to understand which platforms and communities our testers come from. We don't sell your data and we don't use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores the applications in a database.
  • Vercel hosts this website. Vercel Web Analytics counts page visits in aggregate, without cookies.
  • Discord: a copy of each application is posted to a private channel that only the developer can see.

How long we keep it

Until the closed beta ends. After that we delete the applications, unless you've become a tester and agreed to stay in touch for later test rounds.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your application. Send a message to @dev.axon on Instagram with the Discord username you applied with, and we'll handle it within 30 days.

Age

The beta is meant for people aged 16 and over. Please don't apply if you're younger.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
